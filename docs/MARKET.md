# Market Analysis & Go-To-Market Strategy

## Market Context

### The AI Agent Economy is Exploding

- **x402 processed 100M+ payments** since May 2025
- **$380K+ daily payment volume** on Solana via x402
- **10,780% growth** in x402 transactions (Oct 2025)
- Prediction: **90% of on-chain transactions** will be AI-initiated by end of 2025

### The Problem We Solve

As AI agents spend money autonomously, businesses have:
- ❌ No real-time visibility into agent behavior
- ❌ No alerts when agents go rogue
- ❌ No compliance/audit capabilities
- ❌ No emergency stop mechanisms

**One compromised agent = catastrophic financial loss**
